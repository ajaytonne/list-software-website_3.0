Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026

Who we are

LIST Software Pvt. Ltd. (“LIST”, “we”) builds CUSTODIAN core banking software for co-operative banks and credit societies. This policy explains how we handle personal information collected through this website.

What we collect

Details you submit in our forms or chat: name, bank or organisation, phone, email, number of branches, current software, your message, and for career applications the details and CV link you send. If you accept analytics cookies we also measure page visits in aggregate.

Why we use it

To reply to enquiries, arrange demos and callbacks, handle job applications, improve this website and meet legal obligations. We do not sell your personal information.

Who sees it

Our sales, support and HR teams, and service providers that help us run the website (hosting, email, spreadsheets). They may use it only to provide those services. We may disclose information where the law requires it.

How long we keep it

Only as long as needed for the purpose it was collected, after which it is deleted or anonymised.

Your rights

You may ask us to access, correct or delete your personal information, withdraw consent, or raise a grievance, as provided under India’s Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023. Write to bankingsales@listspl.co.in.

Cookies

Essential storage is used for site features such as your theme choice. Analytics runs only if you accept it in the cookie banner.

Changes

We may update this policy; the date below shows the latest version.
