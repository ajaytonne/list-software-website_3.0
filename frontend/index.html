FAQ

Questions bank leaders often ask

What is a browser-less core banking system?

In CUSTODIAN, the application and the data sit in a central data center. Branches connect to it over secure networks, so there is nothing to maintain in each branch’s web browser and policies stay consistent across the bank.

How long does implementation take?

It depends on the number of branches, the data to be migrated and the bank’s configuration needs. After a discovery study we share a realistic timeline for your bank rather than a one-size-fits-all number.

How is data migration handled?

Existing data is mapped, migrated and tested before go-live. Our team has experience migrating banks from other core banking software, and the process includes validation with your staff.

How is security handled?

CUSTODIAN is designed with role-based access control, encrypted data transmission and audit logs. Data and application stay centralized in a monitored data center instead of being spread across branches.

Can CUSTODIAN integrate with ATMs?

Yes. CUSTODIAN supports ATM and EFT switch interfaces, along with other delivery channels such as mobile banking, internet banking, POS and KIOSK. Specific scope is confirmed during consultation.

What about disaster recovery?

A central data center makes it practical to plan a disaster-recovery site and business-continuity process. LIST also offers data center and DR infrastructure setup services.

What support do we receive after go-live?

Dedicated implementation and support engineers help your teams with training, queries and continuous improvement after go-live.

How does pricing work?

Pricing depends on bank size, branches, modules and infrastructure. Book a demo or consultation and we will prepare a quote based on your requirements.
